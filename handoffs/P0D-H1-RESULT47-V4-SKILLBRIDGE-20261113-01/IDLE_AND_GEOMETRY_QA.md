Roblox Creator Hub `Player.Idled` official documentation explicitly says clients are **automatically disconnected after approximately 20 minutes of inactivity**, while `Idled` fires only on the client. https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Player

**Impact:** V4's proposed 30-minute selected focus cannot complete under fully unattended, continuously idle activity, and current session-only XP/focus resets on disconnect. Claiming 30-minute passive free training without persistence is misleading. Do **not** bypass idle policy by artificial input or pretend app remains present.

- `server/V4TrainingBridge` uses current Roblox-authoritative yielding `Player:IsFriendsWithAsync` inside isolated `task.spawn`+protected `pcall`, not deprecated `IsFriendsWith`.
