# TOY WARS FACTORY — product vision (draft)

## One-sentence promise
Design and build toy soldiers on visible production lines, send them straight into battles, and win through manufacturing strategy, teamwork and match-limited blueprint captures.

## World
The **ゼンマイ諸島 (Wind-up Isles)** are a playful archipelago where discarded and new toys awaken at night. The player is a Factory Master who protects the workshop, expands manufacturing and recovers blueprint capsules from rivals. No realistic violence or gore.

## Player loop
Choose toy recipe → physical assembly → automatic deployment → unit battle → match reward → improve factory speed / lineup → retry / play with friends.

## Initial toy roster
- **ポン / PON**: speedy scout and blueprint courier; inexpensive, fragile; turquoise, antenna + round head.
- **ガードン / GUARDON**: shield guardian; slow, durable, low damage; blue, wide blocky shield silhouette.
- **タンクル / TANKURU**: ranged tin tank; slow, expensive, high single-target damage; orange, long barrel and treads.
- **JUNK**: NPC attacker, not player selectable.

## Modes / truthful status
- PvE: offline-tested prototype source exists in local v2.3 archive, not yet fully migrated to this repository.
- PvP: **planned**. 1v1 / 2v2 with contestable **match-only** blueprints and reversible ownership; no permanent inventory theft.
- Co-op: **planned**. 2–4 players defend and specialize their production.
- Saves, economy, monetization, matchmaking: not implemented.

## Differentiation hypothesis
Factory throughput and production scheduling visibly change the composition of the real battlefront. Competitive features are not proven differentiated: test with players before spending on long-tail content.

## Acceptance and anti-fraud
A prototype is not a published game. Actual Roblox runtime logs, cross-client matches, mobile screenshots, visuals and retention evidence are needed for release.
